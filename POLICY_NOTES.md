# Revision Director VA7.1 — Pass D policy notes

## Duration learning

- Only completed cycles within broad plausibility bounds influence estimates.
- Exact-obligation evidence is strongest.
- Same-unit/same-workflow and global-workflow evidence are weaker fallbacks.
- Recent samples receive greater weight.
- Weighted medians reduce outlier sensitivity.
- A per-review movement bound prevents abrupt changes; repeated consistent
  evidence may still correct an inaccurate estimate substantially.
- Split-child time is aggregated with final synthesis before learning.
- Pauses and interruptions are not yet classified, so sessions left open for
  unrelated activity should not be treated as reliable timing evidence.

## Weekly balance

- Uses completed actual minutes from the latest seven configured study days.
- Respects the 4 a.m. boundary and separates subjects by exam profile.
- Target shares reflect importance-weighted active syllabus exposure.
- The signal remains neutral until at least 30 minutes of weekly execution data
  exists.
- Balance is deliberately gentle; due risk, weakness and examination proximity
  remain stronger.

## Interleaving

- Reorders only items within eight score points of the current top candidate.
- Penalises immediate repetition of the same profile-qualified subject and the
  same workflow.
- Active relearning and severe overdue work are not displaced.

## Automatic splitting

- Triggered only for active, planning-eligible obligations exceeding capacity.
- Reserves a bounded part of the parent effort for final synthesis.
- Uses natural page/question boundaries when detected; otherwise creates
  proportional, explicitly labelled parts.
- Scheduling remains parent-owned.
- Untouched cycles may reflow after capacity changes.
- Partially completed cycles do not reflow automatically because changing their
  scope would damage auditability and continuity.

## Persisted plans

- The original recommendation is never overwritten.
- Capacity, order, disposition, estimated duration and rationale are part of the
  material plan snapshot.
- Manual regenerations and manual additions are explicit versions.
- Future session items remain planned; only the current target becomes started.
- Actual execution is stored as outcomes, not written back into the proposal.

## Scientific boundary

These mechanisms improve operational accuracy and revision coverage, but the
planner weights and non-FSRS policies are not yet calibrated outcome models for
IFoS or USMLE marks. They remain transparent, testable baseline policies.
